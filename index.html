FAQ

Quick answers.

What makes Wave different?

Wave is a real-time nightlife hub that combines live videos, social feeds, and venue events in one place. Users and venues post live videos, students can share anonymously or publicly, and venues can post events and drink specials directly to reach groups. We're digitizing the promoter process and creating a complete digital ecosystem for nightlife.

How do live videos work?

Both users and venues can post live videos directly on the app to showcase what's happening at their current spot. See the real vibe, crowd, and energy in real-time before you commit to going out. It's authentic, unfiltered, and happening right now.

Can I post anonymously on the social feed?

Yes. Wave's social feed lets you post under your name or anonymously. Share honest takes, ask questions, connect with your campus or city, and see what's happening — all while having the option to stay anonymous when you want.

How do venues post events and drink specials?

Venues can post events and drink specials directly to the app to reach groups of people who are actively looking for what they offer. This digitizes the traditional promoter process, making it easier for venues to connect with their audience and for people to discover what's happening.

What payment features are coming?

We're building out payment features that will allow venues to list events, sell table purchases for clubs, and process bookings directly through the app. This will help venues capitalize on the digital nightlife experience and make it easier for customers to book experiences.

Where is Wave available?

Wave is starting at Ohio State with plans to expand to other college towns and major cities with heavy nightlife scenes (clubs, bars, etc.). We're building a digital hub that works for any city or campus where people go out.
